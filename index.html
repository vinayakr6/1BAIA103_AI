Course Outcomes & Assessment Alignment

Only wording supported by the supplied assessment material is stated as an official outcome here.

CO1

AI concepts and types

Explain the concepts and types of Artificial Intelligence.

The supplied CIE/model-question material also assesses AI definition, working, advantages/disadvantages, intelligence, agents and search.

CO2

Basic machine learning methods

Illustrate basic machine learning methods for regression, classification and clustering.

The model question paper uses CO2 for Module 3 questions on neural networks, ML models, supervised/unsupervised learning, K-Means and regression.

Why Virtual Labs?

Instead of only reading about AI, students can change inputs, run algorithms, observe results, compare methods, make mistakes safely, repeat experiments and solve challenges. The goal is to make the reasoning behind an AI method visible and testable.
