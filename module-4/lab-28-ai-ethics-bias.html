AI Ethics & Bias Laboratory

Examine fairness, bias, ethical choices and the effect of data or decision rules on AI outcomes.

Learning Objectives

  • Define AI ethics and bias
  • Identify a biased outcome
  • Compare fairness before and after changing data
  • Explain why ethical design matters
How to use: Read the theory, run the simulator, change one parameter at a time, observe the output, then complete the quiz and reflection. These activities are educational simulations, not production AI systems.

Theory

AI ethics concerns responsible development and use of AI. The supplied material discusses ethical use, machine ethics, AI bias, fairness and the importance of ethical and trustworthy AI.
