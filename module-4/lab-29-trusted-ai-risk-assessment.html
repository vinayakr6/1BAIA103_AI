Learning Objectives

  • Identify AI risks
  • Rate risk severity
  • Apply trust criteria

How to use: Read the theory, run the simulator, change one parameter at a time, observe the output, then complete the quiz and reflection. These activities are educational simulations, not production AI systems.

Theory
