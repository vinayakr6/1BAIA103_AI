1. Theory

What is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the field concerned with making intelligent machines, especially intelligent computer programs. AI enables machines to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence, such as learning from experience, recognizing patterns, understanding language, making decisions and solving problems.

Student takeaway: AI is not simply a computer doing a calculation. It involves computational methods that support intelligent tasks.

How does AI work?

The course material describes AI systems as working effectively with data, from which patterns or correlations can be learned and then used for predictions or decisions.

  1. Learning — acquire data and create rules/algorithms for useful information.
  2. Reasoning — choose and use an appropriate algorithm to reach an outcome.
  3. Self-correction — improve algorithms/results toward greater accuracy.

Applications

AI can support recognition, prediction, decision-making, automation and interaction across many domains.

Data matters

Large amounts of data can help identify patterns; incomplete or inaccurate data can produce poor or harmful results.

Human role

People remain important for defining objectives, evaluating results and ensuring responsible use.

3. Advantages & Disadvantages

Advantages

  • Performs well on tasks using detailed data.
  • Processes huge volumes of data in less time.
  • Can generate consistent and accurate results for suitable tasks.
  • Can be used 24 × 7.
  • Can optimize tasks and resource use.
  • Can automate complex processes.
  • Can predict maintenance needs and minimize downtime.
  • Can support new products with better quality and speed.

Disadvantages

  • Can involve more cost.
  • Technical expertise is required.
  • There may be a lack of trained professionals.
  • Incomplete or inaccurate data can lead to disastrous results.
  • AI can lack the capability to generalize tasks.
