Module Theory Map

Topics follow the supplied VTU course material.

01 · AI Foundations

Artificial Intelligence

Definition, how AI works, advantages and disadvantages, and history.

02 · Types of AI

AI Categories

Weak AI, Strong AI, Reactive Machines, Limited Memory, Theory of Mind and Self-Awareness.

03 · Related Concepts

AI, Augmented Intelligence & Cognitive Computing

Compare the concepts and introduce Machine Learning and Deep Learning.

04 · Intelligence

Machine Intelligence

Defining intelligence, components of intelligence, and human versus machine intelligence.

05 · Agents

Agent & Environment

Understand agents, environments and their interaction.

06 · Uninformed Search

Search Algorithms

DFS, DLS, BFS, UCS, IDDFS and Bidirectional Search.

07 · Informed Search

Heuristic Search

Pure Heuristic Search and Best-First Search (Greedy Search).

08 · Knowledge

Knowledge Representation

Introduction, knowledge-based agents and types of knowledge.

09 · Practice

Experiment & Reflect

Change inputs, observe results, solve challenges and explain findings.

  2. Read theory and instructions.
  3. Run the experiment.
  4. Change one input at a time.
  1. Observe and explain the output.
  2. Try the challenge.
  3. Complete the quiz.
  4. Write the reflection and mark completion.
